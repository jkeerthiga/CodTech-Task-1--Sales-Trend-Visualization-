**Project Name:** Sales Trend Visualization


**Project Scope:** Analyzed 3,400 fashion retail transactions to identify sales trends, top-performing products, seasonal patterns, and payment preferences, concluding with actionable business recommendations.

## Methodology
- Cleaned missing values, standardized date formats, removed duplicates
- Calculated summary statistics: total revenue, average purchase, unique customers
- Visualized monthly sales trends, top 10 items, payment method split, and rating by item

## Key Findings
- Tunic, Pajamas, Jeans, Shorts, and Handbag are the top 5 revenue drivers
- Credit card and cash usage is nearly split (52%/48%)
- December is the strongest sales month
